# Bot4VPS Naming and Branding Policy

## Official project

**Bot4VPS** is the official name of this project.

The official maintainer and author is **crashdmd**. The official repository is:

https://github.com/crashdmd/Bot4VPS

## Forks and modifications

Forks and modifications are permitted under the project [LICENSE](LICENSE). A fork or modification does not become an official Bot4VPS version merely by existing.

The existence of a GitHub fork alone does not mean that it is affiliated with the official project, maintained by crashdmd, supported by crashdmd, or endorsed by crashdmd.

Without express permission, third-party versions must not imply official support, affiliation, maintenance, or endorsement by crashdmd.

## Naming third-party versions

Acceptable descriptions for a third-party version include:

- `A fork of Bot4VPS`
- `An unofficial modified version of Bot4VPS`
- `Based on Bot4VPS`

Without express permission, third-party versions must not use branding or claims such as:

- `Official Bot4VPS`
- `Bot4VPS Official`
- `Official Bot4VPS Release`
- `Bot4VPS maintained by crashdmd`

They also must not use other wording that could reasonably make users believe that a third-party version is the official project or is supported or endorsed by crashdmd.

## Logo and visual branding

The same distinction applies to the Bot4VPS logo and other visual branding. A fork may identify its relationship to Bot4VPS factually, but must not use the logo or visual identity in a way that suggests it is an official Bot4VPS release or that it has official support or endorsement without express permission.

## Attribution

Derivative distributions must retain all attribution and other notices required by the [LICENSE](LICENSE). Where practical, link to the official repository so users can identify the original project:

https://github.com/crashdmd/Bot4VPS

This policy does not prohibit forks or modifications. Its purpose is only to distinguish the official Bot4VPS project from independent forks and modified versions.
